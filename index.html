Our History
Nearly 30 Years
of Neighbor Care
"We exist to eliminate hunger and feed the culture of the Third Ward — neighbor by neighbor."

Manna House Third Ward Food Pantry was founded on May 16, 1997 by a coalition of four Houston-area churches united by a single belief: that no one in the Third Ward should go without food. What began as a small community initiative has grown into one of the neighborhood's most trusted resources — open three days a week, every week, for nearly three decades.

Originally located at 3209 Ennis, the pantry eventually moved to its current home at 3118½ Blodgett Street — on the campus of Pilgrim Congregational United Church of Christ. From this location, our all-volunteer team serves 100 or more families each week, providing groceries, canned goods, fresh items, and the dignity of being seen and served.

Manna House is a proud partner of the Houston Food Bank, participating in the annual Share Your Birthday campaign and connecting our community to food resources across the greater Houston area.

🌾
Our Mission
Eliminating hunger. Feeding the culture.
Connecting our community.
Manna House is more than a food distribution center — it is a gathering point for community members, volunteers, and supporters who believe that hunger has no place in the Third Ward. Every person who walks through our doors is treated with respect, warmth, and the dignity they deserve.

The Process
Four Simple Steps
From the moment you arrive to the moment you leave, our volunteers are here to help. Here's what to expect.
1
Come During Open Hours
Visit us at 3118½ Blodgett Street during our open times. No appointment needed. Walk-ins are always welcome. If you've never come before, we'll walk you through everything.
2
Sign In With Our Team
A volunteer will greet you at the door and guide you through our simple sign-in process. An ID is helpful if you have one — but please don't let that stop you from coming. Everyone is welcome.
3
Browse Our Pantry
With help from our volunteers, you'll select food items from our stocked shelves. We carry canned goods, dry staples, beverages, and fresh items when they're available.
4
Take Home What You Need
Our goal is to make sure every household leaves with enough. We serve families of all sizes. Bring a bag or box to carry your items — or we'll help you carry out.
Who We Serve
You Are Welcome Here
Manna House exists for anyone in need. Here's everything you need to know about who can come and what to bring.
🗺️
Who Can Come?
All Harris County residents in need are welcome. Our primary service area is zip codes 77004 and 77021 — but we never turn anyone away based on where they live.
👜
What to Bring
Bring a bag or box to carry your food. An ID is helpful for our records but is never required. Please come as you are — we are glad you're here.
🌐
Languages Spoken
Our team speaks English and Spanish. Hablamos español. If you need language support, just let a volunteer know and we'll connect you with the right person.
Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk-ins are welcome during all open hours. You do not need to call ahead or schedule anything in advance.
Is there an income requirement?
No. We serve all Harris County residents who are in need. There is no income verification or eligibility test required.
How often can I come?
You are welcome to visit as often as you need. Our pantry is open every Thursday, Friday, and the 3rd Saturday of each month.
What if I don't have an ID?
Come anyway. We may ask for your name and some basic information for our records, but an ID is never required to receive food.

Mobile Design Principles
Hours always visibleThe most common mobile visit: checking hours before leaving home. Hours appear immediately below the hero — never buried.

Buttons stacked verticallyOn mobile, CTAs stack so they're large enough to tap. Primary action (Get Help) is always first.

Cards, not columnsMulti-column layouts collapse into easy-scroll single-column cards. No horizontal scrolling anywhere.
Accessibility on Mobile
All tap targets are a minimum 44×44px per WCAG guidelines. Text is minimum 14px at base size. Color contrast ratios exceed 4.5:1 on all body text.

The site is fully compatible with VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android) screen readers. Language switcher is prominent and keyboard accessible.
